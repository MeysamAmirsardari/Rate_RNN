# Supplementary Figure 3 — caption draft

**Supplementary Figure 3 | A learned transition pre-activates its target and
recruits inhibition that suppresses the target on arrival.**

**a**, Mean population rates for the same physical AB sequence after exposure
to two contexts: AB frequent (85 late AB trials; A→B learned) and AB rare
(8 late AB trials; A→B not learned). Shading marks tones A and B; vertical
dashed lines mark the four prespecified circuit snapshots. Solid and dashed
traces denote channels A and B, respectively.

**b,c**, Circuit state at each snapshot after frequent (**b**) or rare (**c**)
AB exposure. Upper and lower nodes are excitatory populations and their
channel-matched interneurons. Node fill is the instantaneous simulated rate;
plastic recurrent E→E line width is the learned weight. Fixed E→I and I→E
connections retain constant width. Excitatory and inhibitory nodes use the two
separately labelled positive-only scales at right.

**d**, Signed identity-controlled contrast, AB frequent minus AB rare, at the
same snapshots. Node fill is the rate difference: red indicates frequent >
rare, blue frequent < rare, and white no difference. Excitatory populations
are warm-outlined circles and inhibitory populations are blue-outlined rounded
squares, so neuron class remains visible independently of sign. Recurrent E→E
connections remain warm; width denotes difference magnitude and solid versus
dashed style denotes sign. Excitatory and inhibitory rate differences use
separate symmetric zero-centred ColorBrewer RdBu scales. The learned A→B link
first raises E_B during tone A (+1.17 a.u. at
45 ms), then leaves I_B elevated in the silent gap (+0.181 a.u. at 65 ms),
before the target response reverses sign during tone B (ΔE_B = -0.33 a.u. at
105 ms).

**e**, Quantification of the cascade. Paired bars show, from left to right:
learned A→B weight,
peak B-channel pre-activation during tone A, mean B-channel inhibition in the
30-ms gap, peak B-channel target response during tone B (expanded axis, explicitly
broken at the origin), the complete rare-minus-frequent E_B time course with
the prespecified tone-B window shaded, and suppression across six independent
seed pairs. Every seed pair showed positive suppression (3.25 ± 0.03%, mean ±
s.e.m.). All values are direct simulator outputs; no smoothing or value
selection is applied.
