# Supplementary Figure 1 | Global field power captures repetition-dependent ECoG responses.

Global field power (GFP; spatial standard deviation across all 32
baseline-SD-normalized channel ERPs after block averaging) for the first and
fifteenth presentations of the variable tone, shown separately for its three
sequence positions. Peach shading marks the 0-180-ms variable-tone window.
Horizontal bars denote intervals where the Rep-1 minus Rep-15 GFP contrast
survived a two-sided whole-block sign-flip cluster-mass test (4,999
randomizations), with family-wise error controlled jointly over all displayed
latencies and recordings. Bar colour indicates the direction of the contrast.
The three recordings were obtained from one animal; inference is therefore
within recording and does not establish an animal-population effect.
